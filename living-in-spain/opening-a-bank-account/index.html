Open the practical guide

The page with the document pack, resident versus non-resident accounts, fees and a real branch example is Opening a bank account in Spain.

  • Banks often ask for a passport, NIE or TIE if you already have one, padrón or other address proof, and income evidence.
  • A non-resident account is sometimes possible with fewer documents, and with more limits.
  • Revolut and Wise are fine for many everyday payments, but a landlord or employer may still insist on a Spanish IBAN.

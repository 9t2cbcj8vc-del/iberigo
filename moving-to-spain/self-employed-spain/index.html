Self-Employed and Autónomo in Spain

A practical starting point for understanding self-employment in Spain, what you may need to prepare, and which tax, Social Security and residence questions to check early.

8 min read

Quick Answer

Self-employment in Spain is often connected to autónomo registration, tax obligations and Social Security. EU citizens and non-EU citizens may face different residence questions. Non-EU citizens usually need the correct residence/work route before carrying out self-employed activity. Autónomo registration is not the same as immigration permission. This roadmap helps you understand the general journey — it does not replace official requirements.

Autónomo basics

Working as autónomo (self-employed) in Spain usually means two registrations before you start invoicing:

  1. Tax census (Hacienda) — file Modelo 036 to register in the census of entrepreneurs, professionals and withholders before you start the activity. (The simplified Modelo 037 was withdrawn in February 2025; use 036.)
  2. Social Security (RETA) — register in the Régimen Especial de Trabajadores Autónomos through Import@ss (Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social).

Reduced cuota (“tarifa plana”): new autónomos (or those returning after a long enough gap) can request a reduced Social Security cuota when they register in RETA. Conditions and the current monthly amount are set by Seguridad Social and applied in the Import@ss alta — check the official RETA / cuota reducida page rather than an old euro figure.

Ongoing obligations typically include invoices, periodic tax filings, RETA contributions and record-keeping. Many people use a gestor; this page is an orientation, not tax advice.

Real Questions People Ask

What does autónomo mean?

Autónomo usually refers to self-employed status in Spain, generally connected to tax and Social Security registration. Exact obligations depend on your activity.

Is autónomo the same as permission to live in Spain?

No. Autónomo is a tax and Social Security registration status. It is not the same as immigration permission.

Do self-employed people pay Social Security?

Self-employment is often connected to Social Security registration and contributions, but exact obligations depend on your situation.

Do I need a gestor?

Not everyone does, but many self-employed people use a gestor or tax adviser to manage registration, filings and contributions.

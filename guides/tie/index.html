TIE: your card filing checklist

National Police procedure · appointment labels and supporting-document checks can vary

Use this for the physical TIE step once you have the visa, residence/stay authorization or favorable decision that supports your card. The TIE documents an existing legal status; it does not create that status by itself.

Procedure

Solicitud de Tarjeta de Identidad de Extranjero (TIE) — form EX-17 for the card-issuance step. Confirm that your underlying authorization or status is already in place.

Where to go

The TIE application is handled in person through the competent Oficina de Extranjería or Policía Nacional office for the procedure. Follow the office shown for your province and appointment.

What to select

In the appointment system, choose the TIE / fingerprinting (toma de huellas) option offered for your card type and province. Do not select NIE assignment or EU-citizen registration instead; wording can differ between offices.

Forms and fee

Prepare EX-17 and proof of payment of Modelo 790, código 012 for the applicable TIE card line. Check the current official fee and the line that matches your card before paying.

Bring
  • Valid passport or travel document required for your case
  • Completed EX-17
  • Proof of payment of Modelo 790 código 012
  • Your favorable resolution, visa or other evidence of the authorization/status supporting the TIE, as applicable
  • Appointment confirmation
  • Any photograph, address evidence or additional documents required by the Police office for your specific card type
What happens after

The Police complete the identification/card-issuance step and give you the instructions for collection or the next step. Follow the collection instructions from that office; the TIE reflects the validity of the underlying authorization.

TIE card: quick answers

When do I book the TIE fingerprint appointment?

Deadline: apply within one month of entering Spain, or of the authorisation being granted or taking effect (Migraciones information sheet HI92 — official TIE / HI92 page). Book the fingerprint (toma de huellas) appointment as soon as you can; what matters is starting the application inside that month. The TIE documents an authorisation that already exists — it does not create it.

What fee do I pay?

Pay Modelo 790 código 012 before the appointment. The amount depends on the authorisation the card documents — check the current official fee on the 790-012 official form rather than relying on an old figure.

Which option do I choose in the appointment system?

Choose the TIE / fingerprinting (toma de huellas) option offered for your card type and province. Do not select NIE assignment or EU-citizen registration; the exact wording can differ between offices.

What do I bring to the appointment?

Your passport, the completed EX-17 form, proof of payment of Modelo 790 código 012, the visa or resolution behind your card, the appointment confirmation, and any photo or address evidence the office asks for.

Is the TIE the same as the NIE?

No. The NIE is your foreigner identity number. The TIE is the physical card that shows that number together with your authorization.

Documents that often slow people down

Identity and civil records

  • Valid passport or EU national ID.
  • Birth, marriage, or partnership records where relevant.
  • Legalization or Hague apostille for many foreign public documents.
  • Sworn Spanish translations when documents are not accepted as-is.

Spain-side basics

  • Address in Spain and, when requested, padrón certificate.
  • Immigration appointment confirmation.
  • Correct EX form for your procedure.
  • Paid tasa receipt, often Modelo 790 with the matching code.

Money, health, purpose

  • Proof of economic means for routes that require it.
  • Health insurance or public coverage proof where required.
  • School admission, remote-work evidence, family link, or permit approval.
  • Criminal record certificate for some visas and residence routes.

Not every document applies to every route. The guide should help you narrow this down.

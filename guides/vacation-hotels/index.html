Spanish Hotel Chains: List of the Biggest Groups (2026)

Meliá, Barceló, Eurostars, NH, RIU, Iberostar and Paradores: which groups are Spanish, rooms in Spain where published, and official sites. The largest Spanish hotel groups by rooms in Spain are Meliá Hotels International (33,903 rooms in 139 hotels), Barceló Hotel Group (21,399 rooms) and Eurostars Hotel Company, part of Grupo Hotusa (18,058 rooms). That order is the Hosteltur 2025 ranking of Spanish chains, with figures collected up to mid-2025. A March 2026 Horwath HTL count of every operator in Spain, including foreign groups, still puts those three first, then Marriott, Accor and Hyatt. NH is not in Hosteltur’s Spanish ranking.

Spanish hotel chains: quick answers

What is the biggest hotel chain in Spain?

By rooms in Spain, among Spanish groups, Meliá Hotels International is first: 33,903 rooms and 139 hotels, then Barceló (21,399 rooms) and Eurostars, part of Grupo Hotusa (18,058). Source: Hosteltur 2025, data to mid-2025. Horwath’s March 2026 count of all operators also ranks Meliá first (30,706 rooms, 122 hotels), then Barceló and Eurostars, then Marriott and Accor. Do not merge the two counts.

Which hotel chains are Spanish?

Hosteltur’s Spain top 10 is Meliá, Barceló, Eurostars, Best Hotels, RIU, H10, Hoteles Globales, Iberostar, Grupotel and Ona. Worldwide, Catalonia, Palladium and Grupo Piñero also sit in the Spanish top 10. Paradores is the state-owned network and is not in that room ranking.

Are NH, Marriott and Hilton Spanish?

No. Marriott, Accor and Hyatt are international groups with published Spain room counts in the Horwath report. Hosteltur removed Minor Hotels Europe & Americas (the NH company) from its Spanish ranking after it delisted in Spain. Hilton operates in Spain, but neither source published a Hilton room count, so none is shown here.

What are Paradores?

Paradores is Spain’s state-owned hotel network, often in historic buildings or nature settings. It is not in the Hosteltur room ranking used above, so this page does not give a hotel or room count for it. Book on paradores.es.

Is it cheaper to book directly with the hotel chain?

Not always. Compare the direct rate, cancellation rules and loyalty benefits with booking platforms before you pay.

Documents that often slow people down

Identity and civil records

  • Valid passport or EU national ID.
  • Birth, marriage, or partnership records where relevant.
  • Legalization or Hague apostille for many foreign public documents.
  • Sworn Spanish translations when documents are not accepted as-is.

Spain-side basics

  • Address in Spain and, when requested, padrón certificate.
  • Immigration appointment confirmation.
  • Correct EX form for your procedure.
  • Paid tasa receipt, often Modelo 790 with the matching code.

Money, health, purpose

  • Proof of economic means for routes that require it.
  • Health insurance or public coverage proof where required.
  • School admission, remote-work evidence, family link, or permit approval.
  • Criminal record certificate for some visas and residence routes.

Not every document applies to every route. The guide should help you narrow this down.
